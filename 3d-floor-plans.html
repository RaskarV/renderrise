3D Floor Plans

Professional 3D floor plan services for your project

A 3D floor plan takes a flat 2D layout and shows it as a furnished, easy-to-read space. RenderRise Studio creates 3D floor plans that let buyers, tenants and clients understand room sizes, flow and furniture placement at a glance, even if they have never read an architectural drawing.

Most people find a traditional floor plan hard to interpret. A 3D version removes that barrier. Walls, doors, windows and furniture appear in perspective, so a visitor can see where the sofa fits, how the kitchen connects to the living area and how much usable space a home offers.

Who it is for

3D floor plans are widely used by real estate agents, off-the-plan marketers, property developers, builders and architects who need buyers or clients to understand a layout quickly.

Why it matters

They help listings stand out, reduce questions about layout and scale, and help buyers and tenants make decisions faster. They also give your team a clear way to compare design options.
