How It Works

At RenderRise Studio, we follow a clear and structured workflow to ensure every project is delivered with accuracy, efficiency, and exceptional visual quality.

01

Project Info & Quote

Send drawings, sketches, references. We prepare a detailed quote & timeline.

  • Materials & finishes
  • Color schemes & landscaping
  • Furniture styles & lighting
02

3D Modelling

We build the structural model from drawings. You receive a preview render to confirm composition.

Adjustments made before materials.

03

First Draft Render

Materials, textures, lighting applied. You get the first realistic visualization.

Clear references help accuracy.

04

Revisions & Changes

Provide feedback on first draft. Compile changes with annotations.

We refine every detail to match your expectations.

05

Final Render & Post-Production

High-res render with post-production enhancements. Balanced color & atmosphere.

Delivered in print/web-ready resolution.
