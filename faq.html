Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about working with RenderRise Studio. Click the arrow next to each question to reveal the answer.

What do I need to start a project with RenderRise?
➡️

To begin, simply send us your project brief along with any available materials — CAD drawings, floor plans, 3D models, sketches, references, or mood boards. The more information you provide, the more accurate and efficient the process will be.

How much do your 3D rendering services cost?
➡️

Each project is unique. Pricing depends on complexity, number of views, level of detail, and deadline. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your project.

How does RenderRise ensure high quality?
➡️

At RenderRise, quality comes first. Every project goes through a structured workflow including internal quality checks, milestone previews, and revision rounds to ensure the final result exceeds expectations.

How long does a project take?
➡️

Turnaround time depends on scope:

  • Small projects: 2–4 business days
  • Medium projects: 4–7 business days
  • Large projects: Custom timeline

Urgent delivery is available upon request.

What does “On-time or Free” mean?
➡️

We are confident in our deadlines. If we miss the agreed delivery date due to our fault, you don’t pay. Simple and fair.

Is my project data safe with RenderRise?
➡️

Absolutely. All files and information are handled with strict confidentiality. We’re happy to sign an NDA if required.

How does the process work?
➡️

Our process is simple and transparent:

  1. You send project details
  2. We provide a quote and timeline
  3. You approve and we begin
  4. You review preview renders
  5. Final delivery
How will we communicate during the project?
➡️

You’ll have a dedicated project manager. Communication is available via email, WhatsApp, or your preferred platform to ensure smooth collaboration.

Who owns the final renderings?
➡️

After full payment, you receive full rights to use the final renderings for marketing, presentations, websites, social media, and advertising.

Are revisions included?
➡️

Yes. We include a reasonable number of revisions within the agreed scope to make sure you’re completely satisfied.

Can I pay after delivery?
➡️

We typically require a deposit before starting. Flexible terms may be available for long-term partners.

Do you work with international clients?
➡️

Yes. RenderRise works with architects, developers, designers, and agencies worldwide.

Can we establish a long-term partnership?
➡️

Absolutely. We value ongoing collaborations and offer special conditions for bulk or recurring projects.

Do I need a subscription?
➡️

No subscription required. You can order services project-by-project.

Still have questions?

If you didn't find the answer you were looking for, please reach out to our team. We're happy to help!
