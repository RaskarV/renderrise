Your Privacy Matters

RenderRise Studio Pty Ltd ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including:

  • Name, company name, email address, phone number
  • Project details, drawings, and design information
  • Billing and payment information

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide 3D rendering and visualization services
  • Communicate about projects, quotes, and invoices
  • Improve our services and client experience
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations

3. Disclosure of Information

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Information may be shared with trusted subcontractors (including offshore partners such as India-based teams) strictly for service delivery.
  • We take reasonable steps to ensure subcontractors comply with confidentiality and data protection obligations.

4. Overseas Data Transfers

Your information may be stored or processed outside Australia where required for service delivery. We take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients handle data in a manner consistent with Australian privacy requirements.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from misuse, loss, or unauthorised access.

6. Access & Correction

You may request access to or correction of your personal information by contacting us.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to websites operated by third parties. Those links are provided for convenience and may not remain current or be maintained. Unless expressly stated otherwise, we do not endorse and are not responsible for the content on those linked websites and have no control over or rights in those linked websites.
